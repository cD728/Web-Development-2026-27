This part introduces stands and pretty much forgets ripple which makes me kind of sad, but with the introduction of stands a lot of cool and overpowered abilities appear like the abilitity to create a sun or travel from a reflection to another.
This part ends with DIO being finally killed and Joseph miraculously being brought back to life using the blood that DIO took from him, and the final battle is really cool as it shows what is a very strong ability and the reappering ability of controling time.
