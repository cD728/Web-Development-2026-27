Diamond is Unbreakable

This part is a very interesting one, as it becomes more of a slice of life introduction a lot of characters that become friends with the main cast.
But also this part introduces a lot of annoying characters that have no redeeming characteristics. And one one of them is the main villan of the part Yoshikage Kira a man that didn't just have a very powerful stand that lets him blow up people into nothing, he also with an arrow was able to gain a second ability that kills people that learn about him and manipulate time.
This part is very good, the slice of life elements are very refreshing in the series being introduced to new and weird people that become friends of the protaganists instead of just becoming mostly in the background.
