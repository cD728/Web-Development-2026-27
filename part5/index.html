Vento Aureo

This part of JoJo's also like the last one changes a lot of the vibe of the show, as it becomes very serious and all the enemies of the cast are killed in horrifying ways instead of just being out of a stand or in the hospital.
This part focuses in the wish of Giorno Giovanna of joining the mafia group Passione so that he can overthrow the boss and make it so that they stop dealing drugs to children and other type of bad things.
This part is also the first part of JoJo's that introduces stand stats outside of the normal A-F and N/A, has it introduces the possibility of a stand having a infinity ability. This stand is called Notorious B.I.G and is as far as I know the only enemy stand that remains alive because there is technically no actual way of killing him.
This part has the third stand enemy that has a time manipulating ability but he gets "killed" in the worst way of all JoJo's enemies, even worse than Kars, because Diavolo is dieing in perpetually until the end of the universe.
